Food allergies — important information

Have an allergy or intolerance? Tell our staff before you order. Each dish lists the allergens in its recipe; traces of other allergens cannot be ruled out (cross-contamination risk).

The 14 allergens under EU Regulation 1169/2011: cereals containing gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soybeans, milk, tree nuts (e.g. almonds, hazelnuts, walnuts), celery, mustard, sesame, sulphur dioxide and sulphites, lupin, molluscs.
